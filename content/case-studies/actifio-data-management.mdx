## Context

At Actifio, I worked on enterprise-class SaaS backup and disaster-recovery solutions for Google Cloud and hybrid workloads. The platform supported data management across virtual machines and databases in cloud and on-premises environments.

## The challenge

Managing backup, recovery, cloning, and analytics across different environments can create fragmented workflows. Enterprises need to understand their data protection state and carry out operations without losing track of the systems and workloads involved.

The portfolio identifies high recovery time objectives, recovery point objectives, and cost as concerns the unified platform was intended to address.

## My role

As a Senior UI/UX Designer, I worked with a global team of designers and architects on interface design for the enterprise platform and contributed to its design system.

## Designing complex data workflows

I created visual assets and icons, developed low-fidelity wireframes to establish structure, and refined the experience through high-fidelity prototypes. I also converted high-fidelity designs into HTML and CSS pages for development and collaborated with developers on integration.

The interface work covered the operations used to manage protected systems, including backup, recovery, cloning, and analytics. Shared visual patterns helped keep those workflows consistent across the product suite.

## Solution

The unified platform supports data protection across data-center and public-cloud environments, including backup to Google Cloud. Its interface brings core data-management operations into a connected experience for system administrators.

## Outcomes

The portfolio describes the unified solution as reducing recovery objectives and costs by bringing cloud and on-premises data management together. No specific numerical measurements are supplied.

## Reflection

Enterprise data-management interfaces have to make powerful operations understandable without hiding their complexity. Clear structure and consistent patterns help administrators move between workload views and the actions they need to perform.
