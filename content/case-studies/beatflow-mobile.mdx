## Context

Beatflow is a workflow-management system designed for financial services. Its mobile app gives users a way to review project activity and schedules on iOS.

## The challenge

Financial-services teams need to manage project workflows and access useful reports, but generic tools can be complex and may not adapt well to industry-specific work.

## My role

As a Specialist UX Designer, I designed the iOS interface, created its user flows and wireframes, developed high-fidelity designs, and collaborated with developers on implementation.

## A mobile view of project work

The screens organize project activities and schedules into a format suited to a phone. Workflow details, activity lists, and reporting views support quick review, while mobile-specific interaction patterns make the app usable on iOS devices.

## Solution

Beatflow provides a mobile view of project activity and schedules, tailored to financial-services clients. Its reporting and analytics extend the workflow beyond individual task lists.

## Outcome

The portfolio describes an iOS-only app tailored to financial-services workflows, with clearer access to activities, schedules, reports, and analytics. No quantitative impact data is supplied.

## Reflection

Making a complex workflow usable on mobile requires prioritizing the information people need to check quickly. A focused view of activities and schedules can complement the deeper reporting and management experiences elsewhere in a product.
