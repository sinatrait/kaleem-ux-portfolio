## Context

At SOULAX, I designed the interface for an AI-driven trailer inventory product. The system supports teams managing large trailer inventories and tracking where trailers are in real time.

## Problem

Finding and tracking trailers across a large inventory can require substantial manual searching. The product was intended to make inventory information easier to locate and manage.

## Role

I worked as a Specialist UX Designer, designing the product interface for AI-driven trailer tracking and inventory management.

## Research

The resume summary does not document project-specific research methods or participant findings, so I have not attributed interviews, usability tests, or user quotes to this work.

## Process

The design challenge was to make a data-heavy operational workflow usable while supporting live inventory tracking. The available project material shows a dashboard, mobile task flows, trailer details, and location information.

## Outcomes

The project goal was to reduce the time spent manually searching for trailers. The resume does not provide a measured reduction, so no numerical performance claim is made here.

## Reflection

This project sits at the intersection of real-time operational data and everyday logistics work. The screens show the importance of making inventory status and location information accessible across desktop and mobile workflows.
