## Context

At SOULAX, I designed an interface for lien registration and search, supporting financial institutions through legally complex processes.

## Problem

Lien workflows involve detailed information and consequential steps. The project aimed to make searches more efficient and less error-prone for the institutions using the system.

## Role

I worked as a Specialist UX Designer, designing the user experience for lien registration and search.

## Research

The resume summary does not include project-specific research methods or participant findings. This case study therefore avoids presenting unverified user quotes or research results.

## Process

The provided screens show lien summaries organized by workflow status, alongside VIN search and registration tasks. This interface structure makes the state of records and the next available actions visible.
