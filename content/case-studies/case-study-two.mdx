## Context

At SOULAX, I designed UX for an AI-assisted quote-generation product. The workflow is built around a practical starting point for sales and operations teams: provide customer and request-for-quote details, or attach a CAD or specification file, then work with the generated quote and its underlying assumptions.

## The challenge

A quote can depend on product dimensions, material and board grade, flute, colors, quantity breaks, delivery location, timing, plant, and freight. When these inputs are spread across messages and documents, it is difficult to see whether a proposed price reflects the right request.

The product goal was to help users generate accurate quotes in seconds. The interface therefore needed to make two things work together: the speed of an AI-assisted conversation and the care required to review a commercial proposal.

## My role

As a Specialist UX Designer, I designed the experience for the AI quote-generation workflow, including request intake, quote review, and pricing explanation.

## From request to review

The experience starts with a flexible prompt rather than a long form. A user can describe the request or upload CAD/RFQ material, then continue the conversation as the quote takes shape. Recent quotes and quick stats provide recognizable ways back into ongoing work.

Once a proposal is available, the screen gives its specifications a dedicated place: product, dimensions, board grade, flute, colors, quantity options, destination, and requested date. Keeping these details visible beside the assistant response makes it easier to check that the quote corresponds to the request.

The pricing explanation adds context to the generated numbers. It shows the kinds of inputs behind a price—such as the paper index, make-ready and waste assumptions, freight lane, and contract tier—so the result is not just a figure in a chat bubble.

## Outcome

The design brings request intake, quote specifications, and explainable pricing into one workflow, supporting the product goal of generating accurate quotes in seconds while giving users a clear view of the details behind each proposal.

## Reflection

AI can accelerate a quote, but it does not remove the need for a person to understand what is being proposed. Pairing the conversational assistant with structured specifications and a readable price explanation helps keep the generated result connected to the commercial details behind it.
