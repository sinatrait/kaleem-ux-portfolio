## Context

At SOULAX, I designed UX for an AI quote-generation tool. The work focused on making a complex financial workflow more approachable for people who need to create quotes.

## Problem

Quote generation can involve multiple variables and business rules. The challenge was to translate that complexity into a tool people can use to generate accurate quotes in seconds.

## Role

I worked as a Specialist UX Designer, designing the user experience for the AI quote-generation workflow.
