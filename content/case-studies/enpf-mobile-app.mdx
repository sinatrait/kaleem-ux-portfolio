## Context

The ENPF mobile app brings insurance-related tasks to members on their phones. The portfolio describes work across claims, balances, user details, and related account tasks.

## The challenge

Traditional ways of managing claims, checking balances, and updating personal information could be cumbersome and time-consuming. Members needed a more convenient way to complete those tasks while away from a desktop.

## My role

As a Specialist UX Designer, I designed the mobile experience from user needs and journey mapping through wireframes, high-fidelity screens, and developer collaboration.

## Organizing member tasks

I mapped user journeys and flows to define how members move through the app. The design brings key tasks—requesting a claim, checking balances, and updating details—into a mobile navigation structure with purpose-designed icons.

The screens show a guided claim process with clear steps, relevant information, and confirmation states, alongside account and profile views for ongoing management.

## Solution

The resulting app gives members a mobile place to lodge claims, review balances, update their details, and complete related insurance tasks. The design emphasizes direct navigation and a clear sequence through claim information.

## Outcome

The portfolio describes a more convenient and efficient mobile experience for managing insurance needs, supported by intuitive navigation and a visual interface. It does not provide measured task-time or satisfaction results.

## Reflection

Mobile access matters most when it makes a real task easier to complete. Structuring claims and account management around clear steps helps members use the app for practical needs, not just for checking information.
