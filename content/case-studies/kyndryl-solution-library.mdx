## Context

The Kyndryl Solution Library was designed to help organizations manage and implement use cases through a connected enterprise platform. The work sat alongside Kyndryl's broader portfolio of digital platforms and services.

## The challenge

Organizations needed a more coherent way to capture use-case requirements, find relevant solutions, and shape an implementation. Disconnected tools and duplicated information made it harder to move from an identified need to a suitable solution.

## My role

As a Specialist UX Designer at Persistent Systems, I designed the experience across the use-case intake, solution builder, library, and recommendation engine.

## Connecting the use-case lifecycle

I designed intake flows to capture requirements in a structured way. The Solution Builder then uses those needs as a basis for a tailored solution, while the library provides a place to store and categorize use cases for future discovery.

The recommendation experience helps people explore relevant solutions based on the needs they have captured. Designing these modules as connected parts of a platform supports continuity from initial request through exploration.

## Solution

The platform brings Use Case Intake, Solution Builder, Use Case Library, and Recommendation Engine into one experience, with interfaces designed to connect requirements, reusable use cases, and solution options.

## Outcomes

The portfolio describes a centralized solution for use-case management, with improved efficiency, less redundancy, more connected modules, and better decision support. No quantitative results are provided.

## Reflection

The value of a library depends on how easily people can move from a need to something reusable. Connecting intake, solution creation, and recommendations helps the system serve as part of the workflow rather than a static catalogue.
