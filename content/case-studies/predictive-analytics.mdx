## Context

At SOULAX, I designed predictive analytics dashboards that translate forecasting models into operational workflows. The goal was to help teams move from reactive responses toward proactive maintenance planning.

## Problem

Forecasting is valuable when people can understand what it means for their work and decide what to do next. The design challenge was to present analytical signals in a way that supports operational decisions.

## Role

I worked as a Specialist UX Designer, designing dashboards and workflows that connect predictive information to actionable operations.
