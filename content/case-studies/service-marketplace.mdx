## Context

This responsive marketplace connects people looking for services with providers who can list and deliver them. The work spans discovery and booking experiences as well as provider-facing service listings and marketplace management.

## The challenge

Finding a reliable provider can involve searching across options, comparing what is included, and coordinating the booking. The project brief describes users struggling to discover suitable services and manage bookings efficiently.

The experience also needs to serve both sides of the marketplace: customers exploring and requesting services, and providers presenting their offerings.

## My role

I created the user journeys, flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity designs, iterating on the experience with feedback and working with the development team through implementation.

## From discovery to booking

I structured the journey around the decisions people need to make: find a service, understand the listing, compare options, request a quotation, and manage the booking. Provider listings give the marketplace its service inventory, while responsive layouts keep the same core paths available across devices.

## Solution

The designed marketplace lets providers list services and customers discover, book, request quotations, and subscribe. Detailed listings, secure payments, and booking management support the journey beyond the initial search.

## Outcome

The result is a responsive service marketplace with connected discovery, quotation, booking, payment, and subscription flows. The portfolio does not provide quantified outcome data for this project.

## Reflection

A marketplace experience is only as clear as both sides of the exchange. Helping customers understand and compare services depends on giving providers a useful way to describe what they offer.
