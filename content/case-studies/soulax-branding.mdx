## Context

As Soulax evolved as a technology company, its digital and physical touchpoints needed to feel like parts of one identity. I led a branding revamp spanning the company website, visual system, customer-facing collateral, and office interiors.

## The challenge

The existing brand lacked cohesion and did not fully reflect the company's innovative direction. Different touchpoints needed a clearer relationship, while the website needed to communicate enterprise data solutions in a way that was usable across screen sizes.

## My role

I led the UX and brand redesign, from auditing existing touchpoints through the visual identity, responsive website, collateral, and physical-space extension.

## Bringing the identity together

I began with a UX audit of the brand and its user touchpoints. The work then connected the identity to the experience: a redesigned logo and visual system informed a responsive website, while the same visual language extended to business cards, brochures, ID cards, templates, and office interiors.

## Solution

The responsive website presents Soulax's enterprise data offering with a clear message, product context, and paths to learn more. The brand system carries through online and offline materials, creating a more consistent experience for clients and employees.

## Outcomes

The portfolio reports a 35% increase in website session time and a 22% reduction in bounce rate following the revamp. It also describes improved onboarding efficiency, client conversions, usability, accessibility, and visual consistency.

## Reflection

A brand refresh is most useful when it reaches beyond a logo. Connecting the identity to the website, collateral, and physical environment helped make the change tangible across the ways people encounter the company.
