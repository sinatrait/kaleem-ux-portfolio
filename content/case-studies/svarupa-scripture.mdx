## Context

Svarupa.org brings together spiritual articles and scriptures with tools for scholarly work on Sanskrit grammar and diagrams. I redesigned its digital platforms to balance the authenticity of the subject matter with clear, contemporary interaction patterns.

## The challenge

Visitors needed an easier way to discover and read deep spiritual content. Scholars had a different but related need: a clear workspace for viewing, editing, and analyzing grammatical and diagrammatic representations of scriptures.

## My role

I redesigned the information architecture and UX across the website and scholarly application, mapping workflows for grammar editing, diagram generation, and comparison.

## Two audiences, connected experiences

For general visitors, I reimagined key pages with narrative-driven layouts, clear calls to action, and interaction patterns that support immersive reading. The navigation establishes distinct paths for Articles, Scriptures, Listen, and For You, helping people choose how they want to explore.

For scholars, I structured the workspace around the content being studied. The screens bring scripture text, grammatical information, and related diagrams into view, with version tracking and collaborative features supporting ongoing editorial work.

## Solution

The redesigned experience adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile while keeping the main content and task relationships understandable. Visitors can follow guided discovery paths; scholars can access structured linguistic data, edit grammar, and compare system- and user-generated diagrams.

## Outcomes

The portfolio describes increased time on key scripture pages, stronger scholar engagement with grammar tools, and improved adoption among new contributors following simpler flows. It also reports a 35% increase in session time and a 22% reduction in bounce rate for the website.

## Reflection

One platform can serve very different kinds of attention: a visitor exploring a text and a scholar editing its structure. Giving each audience a clear path, while keeping the content central, helped the experience support both casual discovery and expert work.
