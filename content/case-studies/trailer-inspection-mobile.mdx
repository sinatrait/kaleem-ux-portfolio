## Context

I designed a mobile app to digitize DOT compliance inspections for fleet operations. The experience takes an inspection from trailer identification through checklist completion, documentation, and signature.

## The challenge

Manual inspections could be slow and difficult to track. The portfolio reports a 64% error rate in defect logging and no real-time visibility for fleet managers, making it important to capture inspection details consistently and make progress easier to follow.

## My role

I researched inspection pain points through shadow sessions and journey mapping, then designed and prototyped mobile-first inspection workflows.

## Making inspections easier to complete and review

The inspection flow begins with VIN search and guides the user through dynamic checklists. Progressive disclosure and contextual help keep the task focused as the operator moves through inspection types.

When a defect is recorded, the workflow requires a photo and notes, adding evidence alongside the checklist result. Drafts auto-save with 30-day retention, and inspection progress remains visible to support continuation and fleet oversight.

## Solution

The mobile experience covers trailer lookup, inspection checklists, defect documentation, digital signatures, and saved work. Photo validation and required notes are built into defect reporting rather than left to memory.

## Outcomes

The portfolio reports inspections completed 65% faster and 100% digital audit compliance, compared with 68% for paper-based processes. It also identifies the earlier 64% defect-logging error rate as a problem the digitized workflow set out to address.

## Reflection

Compliance depends on the quality of the record as much as the checklist itself. Requiring evidence for defects, preserving drafts, and making progress visible helps create a more complete digital trail through the inspection.
