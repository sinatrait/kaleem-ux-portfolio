About

I design enterprise systems
that people actually use.

I'm Abdul Kaleem, a Principal UX Designer with 15+ years of experience designing enterprise SaaS platforms, operational workflows, and AI-assisted systems for Fortune 500 companies and high-growth technology firms.

My work lives at the intersection of complexity and clarity. I take systems where the problem is genuinely hard — thousands of data points, multiple user roles, mission-critical decisions — and design interfaces that make them feel manageable, even intuitive.

Over my career, I've led design for analytics dashboards used by hundreds of concurrent users, redesigned AI workflow tools that had been abandoned due to poor adoption, and built design systems that scaled across product portfolios.

I work best in environments where the design challenge is not trivial — where there is real organizational complexity, real technical constraint, and real stakes for the people using what gets built.

Process

How I approach design.

I believe great UX is not about beautiful screens — it's about the quality of thinking that precedes them. My process is research-led, iterative, and deeply collaborative.

01

Understand the system

I start by mapping the full context — people, workflows, data, constraints. I don't start designing until I understand what the system is actually doing.

02

Define the problem precisely

The hardest part of design is often identifying the right problem to solve. I invest heavily in this phase before opening Figma.

03

Explore widely, commit deliberately

I generate multiple structural approaches and test them quickly — then commit to the direction that best serves the real user need.

04

Validate with real users

Testing is not a phase at the end — it's woven through every iteration. I test early, test often, and let findings drive the design forward.
